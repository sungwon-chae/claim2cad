The pintle pin sits on the vertical hinge axis at the origin; the main member U-bracket opens toward -X so its upper and lower extensions straddle the pin, the smaller u_shaped_link_member nests inside the main member opening with its hole on the same axis, and the door-half U-bracket wraps around from the -X side with its leaf flange extending inward over the top to receive the pin. The stop_means projects from the link member to contact the main member, limiting rotation.

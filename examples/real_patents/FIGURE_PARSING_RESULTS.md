## Headline observations

- **Zero** of the 25 patents have inline numbered references in claim 1.
  All mappings come from VLM-extracted labels matched against IR
  component labels.
- The **8 fully-mapped patents** span all USPC classes in our dataset
  (074 gears, 414 manipulators, 16 hinges, 901 robots), so the figure
  parser isn't lopsided toward one mechanism family.
- The **2 zero-mapped patents** had IR components with very abstract /
  formulaic labels ("a stationary inner brake band", "a means for…")
  that don't textually match any VLM description. Future work in
  V1-13 will use figure layout (bbox proximity) as a second matching
  signal.

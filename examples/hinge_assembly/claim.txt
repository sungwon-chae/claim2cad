A four-bar linkage mechanism, comprising: a fixed link; a first rotating link pivotally connected to the fixed link at a first revolute joint; a coupler link pivotally connected to the first rotating link at a second revolute joint; a second rotating link pivotally connected to the coupler link at a third revolute joint and to the fixed link at a fourth revolute joint; wherein the four revolute joints define a planar four-bar linkage that converts rotational motion of the first rotating link into a constrained planar motion of the coupler link.
